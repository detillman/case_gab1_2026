# An intrinsically disordered adaptor protein controls the size and composition of EGFR condensates on membranes
Scripts for investigation of how GAB1 destabilizes EGFR condensates and nucleates distinct assemblies: quantification of condensate stability and composition, identification of condensate-destabilizing sequence features, computational reconstitution of phase separated systems, in silico prediction of PH domain oiligomerization

Information for each script is provided below. Please contact me, or refer to our preprint (), for additional information.

slb_condensateCounting.ijm: quantifying condensate number and area on supported lipid bilayers

slbs_cells_condensates.R: forming and disrupting condensates on supported lipid bilayers and in cells

sequence_features.R: identifying sequence features underlying condensate destabilization

slb_frap.ijm: quantifying condensate FRAP properties on supported lipid bilayers

fov_frap.ijm: quantifying whole-FOV FRAP intensity for background correction

slbs_frap.R: elucidating condensate FRAP properties on supported lipid bilayers

ph_dimers.R: predicting PH-mediated homodimerization across the human proteome

guv_condensateCounting.ijm: quantifying condensate number and area on giant unilamellar vesicles

guvs_condensates.R: forming and disrupting condensates on giant unilamellar vesicles

guv_frap.ijm: quantifying condensate FRAP properties on giant unilamellar vesicles

guvs_frap.R: elucidating condensate FRAP properties on giant unilamellar vesicles
